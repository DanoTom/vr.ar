Which VR headset to gift in 2026: a pick for each person

Updated November 2026 · 5 min read

For most people, a Meta Quest 3S. It needs no console or PC, it's the cheapest way into VR, and it plays the whole Quest library. A PSVR2 only makes sense if they already own a PS5, and a Quest 3 if they play on a gaming PC or care about a sharper image. Below, a pick for each kind of person, with an accessory and a first game.

Prices are US list prices as of November 16, 2026. Early Black Friday deals have already put the Quest 3S at about $297, and Meta hasn't announced its own Black Friday price yet. This guide gets updated after November 27.

A pick for each person

A kid aged 10 to 12

A Quest 3S with 128GB ($349.99). Meta allows it from age 10, but between 10 and 12 a parent has to create and manage the child's account, which comes with daily time limits and app approval. Under 10, Meta says no. Add a silicone face cover: it wipes clean in seconds and handles sweat better than foam. First game: Walkabout Mini Golf, calm and fun for the whole family.

A teenager who plays games

A Quest 3S with 256GB ($449.99), because 128GB fills up quickly once a few big games are installed. Add a head strap with a built-in battery: long sessions get more comfortable and the battery lasts longer. First game: Batman: Arkham Shadow.

Someone who owns a PS5

A PSVR2 ($399), the only headset that works with the console for new buyers. Two caveats: this year it has been hard to find new at list price in the US, and Sony's support for it has thinned out (the details are in our PS5 guide). Sony says it's not for children under 12. It needs no accessories. First game: Gran Turismo 7, whose VR mode is a free update if they already own the game.

Someone with a gaming PC

A Quest 3 ($599.99). It plays its own games and also PC VR games, with a cable or wirelessly, and its lenses are sharper than the 3S's. Add a USB-C Link cable for the most stable connection to the PC; here's how to connect it. First game: Half-Life: Alyx.

Curious parents, or someone who doesn't play games

A Quest 3S with 128GB. What they'll enjoy most is calm: films on a giant screen, 360° videos and travel, mini golf. Start with short sessions and comfortable apps, because motion sickness is more common at the beginning (our motion sickness guide explains how to avoid it). If they wear glasses, they can keep them on, though prescription lens inserts are more comfortable: using VR with glasses.

Someone who already has a Quest

Accessories rather than another headset: a strap with a battery, a silicone face cover or a carrying case. They're all in our Quest 3 accessories guide. A Meta gift card for games works too.

When not to give VR

  • To a child under 10. Meta doesn't allow it, and Sony sets the PSVR2 at 12.
  • To someone who gets carsick or seasick easily and has never tried VR. Better if they try one first, a friend's or one in a store.
  • As a surprise for someone who never showed interest. VR asks for time and space, and an unwanted headset ends up in a drawer.
  • A PSVR2 for someone without a PS5. It doesn't work on its own, and the console adds about $600.
  • Where there's no room to move. You can play seated, but many games want a clear space of about 2 × 2 meters (6.5 × 6.5 ft).

Before buying, check the store's return policy, in case the headset doesn't suit them.
